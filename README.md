# Manufacturing Process Mapping & Loss Analysis

**Manufacturing Engineering Internship | Mondelēz International**  
**Clif Bar Manufacturing Facility | Twin Falls, Idaho | Summer 2026**

## Project Overview

During his Manufacturing Engineering internship at Mondelēz International, Pranav worked on a process engineering project focused on process mapping and loss analysis for Line 3, with particular attention to wrapper and caddy former operations.

The project applied structured manufacturing process analysis to understand production workflows and identify opportunities for operational improvement.

## Project Focus

- Process mapping of Line 3, focusing on wrappers and caddy formers.
- Understanding manufacturing processes and identifying areas of potential loss.
- Developing and presenting an engineering solution to the manufacturing team.

## Project Outcome

Pranav presented his proposed solution and project findings to the plant team at the conclusion of his internship. The team accepted the proposed solution for implementation at the facility.

As the internship had concluded, subsequent implementation was to be carried forward by the plant team. The repository therefore documents the internship project and its accepted proposal, not verified post-implementation results.
